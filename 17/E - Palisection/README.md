Here are examples of such strings: «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">eye</span>», «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">pop</span>», «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">level</span>», «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">aba</span>», «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">deed</span>», «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">racecar</span>», «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">rotor</span>», «<span class="tex-font-style-tt">madam</span>». </p><p>Nick started to look carefully for all palindromes in the text that they were reading in the class.
